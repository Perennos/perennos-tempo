`CASE` is the registered use. Evidence paths name the fixed conformance inputs; independent review resolves restricted supporting references. Records remain In progress until that review. Missing records and unavailable checks are Untested. `operations` points to operating-record references in use-case.json; it is not an eighth input file. Section 11 is explicitly outside software authority.

## Evidence boundary

Signed receipts depend on the separate gap 12 verifier and trusted node identity. No signature is established by a file hash or a supplied flag. Real-model tuning, a successful private-route outage test, fairness and a working human challenge route require their own evidence. The exporter does not turn on a live use, notify a regulator, or infer legal permission.
